About eFootball.fans

Discover the story behind the world's largest international eFootball community

Our Story

eFootball Fans International Community is more than just a gaming community – we're a global family of passionate football enthusiasts and eFootball players who have been connecting hearts and minds across continents since our humble beginnings.

What started as a small group of PES Mobile enthusiasts in late 2018 has evolved into one of the most vibrant and inclusive international eFootball communities on the planet. Our journey from the "PES Mobile International Community (P.M.I.C.)" to eFootball Fans International Community reflects not just the evolution of the game we love, but also our commitment to growing and adapting with our community.

Today, we proudly stand as a thriving hub of over 10,000 members from every corner of the globe, united by our shared passion for football and the beautiful game in all its digital forms.

Our Journey

2018-2019
The Beginning
Founded as "PES Mobile International Community (P.M.I.C.)" - one of the first English-speaking international communities dedicated to bringing PES Mobile players together from around the world.
2019-2020
Growing Community
Expanded our reach across multiple platforms and regions, establishing ourselves as a trusted hub for PES Mobile enthusiasts and football fans worldwide.
2020-2021
Evolution & Adaptation
Transitioned alongside the gaming landscape, evolving from PES to eFootball while maintaining our core mission of connecting passionate players globally.
2021-Present
eFootball.fans Era
Rebranded as eFootball Fans International Community, expanded to multiple platforms including Discord, launched community blogs, and reached 10,000+ members strong.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to create the most inclusive, welcoming, and vibrant international community for football and eFootball enthusiasts.

We believe that football is more than just a game – it's a universal language that connects people across cultures, continents, and backgrounds. Through our digital platforms, we aim to:

• Foster Global Connections: Bring together players from all corners of the world to share their passion for football and gaming.

• Support All Skill Levels: Whether you're a casual fan or a competitive pro, our community provides a space for everyone to learn, grow, and enjoy.

• Share Knowledge & Experiences: Create platforms where community members can share insights, strategies, tips, and personal gaming experiences.

• Stay Current & Informed: Keep our community updated with the latest news, developments, and exclusive content from the world of eFootball and football gaming.

• Build Lasting Friendships: Facilitate meaningful connections that extend beyond gaming, creating a true sense of community and belonging.
